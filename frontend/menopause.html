MANAGING YOUR SYMPTOMS
🔥
Hot Flashes
Dress in layers, avoid spicy food and caffeine, keep a fan nearby, stay hydrated.
😴
Sleep Issues
Keep bedroom cool, avoid screens before bed, light yoga or stretching in the evening.
😊
Mood Changes
Regular exercise, talking to someone you trust, and spending time outdoors can help.
🦴
Joint Pain
Calcium and Vitamin D are important. Gentle yoga, warm compresses, and staying active help.
