🌙 About perimenopause and irregular periods
In perimenopause (typically 40s-50s), periods can become irregular — shorter, longer, heavier, lighter, or skipped entirely. This is completely normal. However, if you bleed after 12 months of no periods, or notice unusually heavy bleeding, please consult your doctor. Tracking your cycle helps you understand your body's changes during this transition.
